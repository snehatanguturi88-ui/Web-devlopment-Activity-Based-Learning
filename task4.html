Library Guidelines

  1. Students must maintain silence in the library.
  2. Return books before the due date.
  3. Do not damage library books.
  4. Keep the library clean.
  5. One student can borrow up to 3 books.
  6. Library books should be handled carefully.
  7. Library membership is required to borrow books.
